Rewards FAQ

Everything about points, tiers and referrals

When are points added to my account?

Purchase points are added once your order is marked Delivered. Bonus points (reviews, birthday, referrals) are added within 24 hours.

How do I use my points?

Convert 500 points or more into a reward coupon on this page (1 point = ৳1), then apply the RWD code at checkout or show it in any of our branches.

Do points expire?

Points stay valid for 12 months from the date they were earned. Reward coupons are valid for 90 days after you create them.

Will redeeming points lower my tier?

No. Your tier is based on lifetime points earned from purchases, so redeeming points never moves you down a tier.

Can I combine reward coupons with other offers?

Reward coupons can be used with regular prices and most offers, but not with Flash Sale prices or other coupon codes on the same order.

Is there a limit on referrals?

No limit. Each friend can only use one referral code, and only on their first order with TechStore BD.
